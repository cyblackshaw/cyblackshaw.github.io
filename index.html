Hurricane Hazards and Risk Analysis Group

I study tropical cyclone and multi-hazard risk under a changing climate, with a focus on extreme winds, coastal flooding, compound urban hazards, and infrastructure resilience.

My research combines physical hazard and climate modeling with uncertainty quantification and risk analysis, connecting tropical cyclone hazard characterization and climate information to engineering applications and urban resilience. I also develop interactive scientific tools that make evolving hazard estimates and climate-sensitive design information easier to explore and communicate.

Research

Hazards, uncertainty, and resilience across timescales

My research spans tropical cyclone hazard and risk modeling, infrastructure resilience, and compound urban hazards. Across these themes, I connect meteorological and climate information to hazard estimates, engineering applications, infrastructure impacts, and decision-relevant tools.

01 · Hazard modeling

Tropical Cyclone Hazard & Risk Modeling

Developing and applying statistical, probabilistic, and physics-based approaches to characterize tropical cyclone hazards across forecasting and climatological timescales.

  • Real-time forecasting: Integrate official tropical cyclone forecasts with wind, rainfall, and storm-tide models while evaluating how uncertainty propagates from meteorological forecasts into hazard estimates.
  • Long-term hazard assessment: Combine paleohurricane records with climatological and hydrodynamic modeling to extend understanding of hurricane and storm-surge risk beyond the historical record.
  • Synthetic tropical cyclones: Develop and evaluate synthetic hurricane modeling approaches for estimating low-probability, high-impact hazards under current and future climates.

02 · Infrastructure resilience

Infrastructure Risk & Resilience

Translating tropical cyclone and climate hazards into assessments of infrastructure vulnerability, design, and resilience.

  • Structural design: Evaluate hurricane wind hazards across climate-model ensembles to understand how changing and uncertain hazards affect structural design winds and engineering requirements.
  • Transportation infrastructure: Model flood propagation and damage in the New York City subway system to evaluate flood-protection strategies under current and future hurricane conditions.
  • Energy systems: Generate tropical cyclone and wind-hazard inputs under current and future climates to support assessment of power-outage risk and energy resilience.

03 · Compound hazards

Compound Urban Hazards

Investigating how tropical cyclone hazards interact with extreme heat and the urban environment to create risks that are missed when hazards are considered independently.

  • Couple atmospheric, urban-canopy, and inundation models to simulate tropical cyclone flooding, winds, and extreme heat.
  • Evaluate post-storm heat stress and hazard interactions at city-block scale.
  • Examine how urban conditions modify compound hazard exposure and implications for community resilience.
